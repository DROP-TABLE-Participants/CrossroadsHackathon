# Dandelion Chocolate knowledge pack v1

Public company information: Dandelion Chocolate is a San Francisco bean-to-bar chocolate maker. Its single-origin dark chocolate is made with cocoa beans and cane sugar. The publicly described process sorts, roasts, cracks and winnows, grinds with sugar, tempers, molds and wraps chocolate.

Products used as inspiration: Maya Mountain, Belize 70%; Kokoa Kamili, Tanzania 70%; Costa Esmeraldas, Ecuador 85%. The demo assumes 57 g bars. Check current public products before treating harvest years as current.

Sources:
- https://www.dandelionchocolate.com/pages/our-story
- https://blog.dandelionchocolate.com/process/
- https://www.dandelionchocolate.com/collections/bars?page=2
- https://www.dandelionchocolate.com/products/maya-mountain-belize-70-2025-harvest-single-origin-chocolate-bar

All suppliers, customers, orders, inventory, recipes, quantities, quality checks and batches are simulated. This is an independent demonstration, not an official Dandelion system or a representation of its internal processes.

Scope: three chocolate products; ingredients and packaging; manually selected lots and quantities; production batches and receipts; quality approval; wholesale orders and shipments; committed stock movements. Presentation story: 600 Belize bars ordered, 200 released bars available, produce 400, approve quality, ship 600. Simulated manual issue quantities for 400 bars: 15.96 kg processed cocoa, 6.84 kg cane sugar and 400 wrappers. No automated MRP, scheduling, accounting or procurement.

Customization generates a separate ERP. Use the visitor requirements and preserve explicit questions or assumptions where details are missing. Do not claim that generated code or seeded transactions have been tested until the normal pipeline verifies them.
